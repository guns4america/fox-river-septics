COMMON QUESTIONS

Good answers start with good questions.

Have a situation that's not covered here? Call or send a few details.

How do I know if my septic system needs attention?

Recurring backups, persistent odors or unusually wet areas can be signs of a problem. A professional assessment can help identify the cause.

Do you work outside Serena?

The business serves several surrounding North Central Illinois communities. Reach out with your location to confirm availability.

Can an older septic system be repaired?

Sometimes repairs are possible; other situations call for larger system work. The right recommendation depends on the condition of the system and site.

What should I include in an estimate request?

Share your location, the type of work you need, any symptoms, and your preferred way to be contacted.
